# JFA Amendment 004 (DRAFT) — Exit at the Core Is Priced, Never Sealed

**Status: draft sketch for Board consideration. Not operative.** Prepared 2026-08-13 during the COSDS curriculum build, at the President's direction, after a steward correction to the course's governance module. The canon is amended in the open, on the membership's timing; this file is an input to that process, not an act of it. Nothing here binds until adopted.

## The occasion

The course had hardened the canon's price tag into a wall. The canon says the specification is "the one layer no one can *cheaply* leave"; curriculum documents had been teaching "the one layer no one can leave." The President's correction, recorded here so the amendment carries its own provenance: **at every level the stack is forkable if liquidity can be mustered — and a community that cannot muster it can still leave to honor a different covenant and federate with the original and any other.** The correction has been applied to the curriculum (the 04-governance module and Article 4 of *The Five Layers*). But two phrases in the canon itself carry the same overstatement, and this draft proposes their repair.

## Amendment One — the one rule's third clause

**The claim.** The core's exit is the dearest in the stack, not an absent one — and the canon already knows it. GOV-2 lists "the expensive-but-real fork of its implementation" among the exactly three things that can discipline the core's steward. Amendment 002 makes fork-capacity a provisioned duty — "fork-capacity is provisioned, not merely permitted" — a duty that would be unintelligible for a layer that could not be left at all. Part I concedes the same: "A license guarantees the right to fork; it cannot supply the capacity" — and the whole broadcast-and-preservation apparatus exists to supply it. A canon that provisions an exit in one part and declares it impossible in another is carrying a contradiction; this amendment resolves it toward the provisioned exit.

**Proposed deltas.**

1. Glossary, *The one rule* — current text: "govern every layer by the cost of leaving it: exit where leaving is cheap, voice where it is dear, standing contestation where it is impossible." Proposed: "govern every layer by the cost of leaving it: exit where leaving is cheap, voice where it is dear, standing contestation where it is **dearest** — priced, never sealed: the fork stays expensive-but-real (GOV-2), fork-capacity stays provisioned (Amendment 002), and federation under a differing covenant remains open to a community that cannot muster the liquidity to fork."
2. Glossary, *Gravity well* — current text: "shallow at the surface, where users drift freely between front ends; bottomless at the core, the spec you contest but cannot exit." Proposed: "shallow at the surface, where users drift freely between front ends; **deepest** at the core, the spec you contest long before you fork — dear, never sealed."
3. Part I, *One rule, one form* — current text: "Where leaving is impossible, keep the layer minimal, keep it legible, and hold it under permanent contest." Proposed: "Where leaving is **dearest**, keep the layer minimal, keep it legible, and hold it under permanent contest — and keep the fork real and federation open, so that even the dearest departure is priced, never barred."
4. Part II, the gravity-well passage — same repair as delta 2 ("bottomless … cannot exit" → "deepest … contest long before you fork").
5. GOV-1, appended note: "Exit at the core is priced, never sealed: the expensive-but-real fork stays provisioned, and **federation under a differing covenant is a conformant departure** — a community that leaves to honor a different covenant may federate with the original and with any other."

**Conformance delta.** The standard's check 5 (*Governed by the cost of leaving*) gains a clause: no conformant document may present the core as exitless; the fork is expensive-but-real, and federation under a differing covenant is a conformant departure, not a defection.

**What this does not change.** The price ordering stands: the core remains the dearest layer to leave, standing contestation remains its primary regime precisely because it is the cheap first resort that makes most forks unnecessary, and the minimal entrenched core, the double lock, and the named steward are untouched. This amendment removes a wall, not a price.

## A note for the same drafting pass

The glossary's one-rule sentence assigns *exit* to where leaving is cheap and *voice* to where it is dear, while GOV-1 and the curriculum's Article 4 assign *voice* to the peer layer (cheap contest) and *exit* to the front ends and substrate (dear, made cheap by the protocol). The readings are reconcilable, but the texts should agree; whichever assignment the Board settles on, the repair belongs in the same pass as the deltas above.
